Flight night: TR 588 leaves at 1:45 AM Wednesday, so after Universal Studios on Tuesday you collect bags at the hotel and head to Changi around 9:30 PM.
Deepavali: Sunday 8 Nov, with Monday 9 Nov a public holiday. Little India (right outside the hotel) will be lit up and very crowded in the evenings. Expect bigger crowds at the Oceanarium.
Toddler tips
  • Rain: November is wet season. Pack a compact umbrella, stroller rain cover and spare clothes.
  • MRT: Lifts at every station. Tap a contactless bank card; your son rides free.
  • Race day: Ear protection, water, hat, snacks.
  • USS: Bring or rent a stroller. Use rider switch to take turns on bigger rides.
  • Flight night: Dinner and pajamas before the airport so he sleeps on board.
