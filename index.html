Flight night: TR 588 leaves at 1:45 AM Wednesday. After Universal Studios on Tuesday, head back to the hotel to pack and rest, then leave for Changi around 9:30 PM.
Diwali: Sunday 8 Nov, with Monday 9 Nov a public holiday. Little India (right outside the hotel) will be lit up and very crowded in the evenings. Expect bigger crowds at the Oceanarium.
Tips for travelling with Aki
  • Rain: November is wet season. Pack a compact umbrella, stroller rain cover and spare clothes.
  • MRT: Lifts at every station. Tap a contactless bank card; Aki rides free.
  • Race day: Ear protection, water, hat, snacks.
  • USS: Bring or rent a stroller. Use rider switch to take turns on bigger rides.
  • Flight night: Dinner and pajamas before the airport so he sleeps on board.
